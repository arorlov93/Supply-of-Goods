**Subject:** RFQ — Presspride Newsprint 45 g/m² in Reels — Regular Monthly Supply to Gaborone, Botswana

Dear Sir / Madam,

**1. Company introduction.** We are [COMPANY NAME], a [trading / distribution] company registered in [COUNTRY], supplying printing and packaging papers to customers in Southern Africa. We are establishing a regular, long-term newsprint supply programme into **Botswana**, delivering to end-users in **Gaborone**. Our company is registered outside Botswana; we are finalising our importer-of-record arrangements and will confirm the named Botswana importer and its BURS Trader Identification Number before the first delivery.

**2. Why we are approaching Sappi directly.** We understand Ngodwana Mill to be the only operational newsprint facility in South Africa and within the Southern African Customs Union. As Botswana is a SACU member, supply from Ngodwana reaches Gaborone as an intra-SACU road movement — no seaport, no bill of lading, no removal-in-bond transit and a single customs action. That is a materially simpler and lower-risk supply chain than the imported alternative, which is why you are our preferred source rather than a fallback.

**3. Destination and routing.** Gaborone, Botswana, by road from Ngodwana via the N4 through Pretoria and Zeerust, entering at Tlokweng Gate or Pioneer Gate. Please advise which border post and routing you or your appointed carrier normally use.

**4. Monthly volume and annual commitment.** **Approximately 40–80 tonnes per month**, on a continuous monthly basis (equivalent to 2–3 x 20ft containers, though see point 9 on loading format). Subject to a satisfactory trial delivery and confirmed print performance, we are prepared to enter a **12-month frame contract with monthly call-offs**, giving you booked annual tonnage rather than individual orders. Please quote on that basis as well as spot.

**5. Product and grade.** **Newsprint Presspride**, reel format, machine finished, virgin fibre, for coldset and heatset web offset printing of newspapers and advertising inserts. Please confirm the furnish and whether any recovered fibre is included.

**6. Grammage.** Our primary requirement is **45 g/m²**, which we understand to be your published Presspride grammage. Please confirm:
- whether **42, 48.8 and 52 g/m²** are also currently available, and if not, whether they can be produced to order and at what minimum run;
- the **grammage tolerance (± g/m²)** you guarantee;
- your **price per tonne for each grammage separately**, as we understand grammage affects price.

Please also clarify the position of **Superpride**: your product listing places it under Newsprint, but we have been unable to obtain its datasheet. Is it a true newsprint, an improved newsprint, or a book/publication grade? We do not wish to quote the wrong product to our customer.

**7. Reel requirements.** Please state, for each available option:
- available **trim / reel widths (mm)**, and which widths carry **no trim surcharge** at our order size;
- **reel diameter (mm)**, maximum and minimum;
- **core inside diameter (mm)** — please confirm whether 76 mm (3") is standard;
- **average and maximum reel weight (kg)**;
- whether reels ship **eye-to-sky or eye-to-side** as standard;
- whether **sheets** can be supplied if required, and at what minimum quantity. (We note your ITAC application refers to "rolls or sheets".)

**8. Technical specification required with your offer.** Please provide the **Presspride mill technical datasheet** and confirm guaranteed values and tolerances for: **ISO brightness; opacity; moisture content (%) with tolerance; ash content (%); bulk / caliper; tensile strength MD and CD; tear strength MD and CD; roughness (Parker Print Surf); porosity; and pH / sizing.** We require a **mill Certificate of Analysis for every reel or production lot delivered.** Please confirm the applicable quality standard and the ISO 9001 certification scope for Ngodwana Mill, and provide your **FSC Chain of Custody / Controlled Wood certificate number** (we note FSC-C019831) together with any price difference for certified material.

**9. Loading format — please advise rather than assume.** Our cargo is weight-limited, not volume-limited. Because this is a road movement inside SACU, please advise **whether flat-deck or tautliner trailer loading is more economical than containerisation for this lane**, and quote whichever you recommend. If you quote containers, please state the **net tonnage of reels you load into a 20ft unit** and the **maximum gross mass you load to**, noting that many in-service 20ft units are CSC plate-rated to 24,000 kg rather than 30,480 kg. Please also describe your standard loading pattern and securing method for reels.

**10. Packaging.** Please confirm your standard reel packaging: moisture-barrier wrapping (please confirm it is fully watertight), edge and end protection, headers, labelling and barcoding, and whether reels are delivered on pallets or floor-loaded.

**11. Incoterms and price requested.** Please quote, **in ZAR per tonne**:
- **Ex-Works Ngodwana Mill**; and, separately,
- **DAP Gaborone, Botswana** (delivered, duty unpaid — we will handle Botswana import clearance and VAT).

Please state the **validity period** of the quotation, any **small-order or trim surcharge** as a separate line, and a **volume price ladder** for 40, 60, 80, 150 and 300 tonnes per month so we can plan growth with you. If you prefer to quote in USD, please do so in addition rather than instead.

**12. Payment terms.** Please state your standard terms for a new account, whether a credit application and trade references are required, what security you would require for open-account terms, and what discount, if any, applies to payment in advance.

**13. Lead time.** Please state the **lead time from order confirmation to despatch**, the road transit time Ngodwana → Gaborone, and the earliest production slot available for a first trial delivery.

**14. Documentation.** Please confirm you will provide: commercial invoice; packing list showing reel-by-reel weights and numbers; delivery note; **non-preferential Certificate of Origin** (required for our Botswana customs declaration even though HS 4801.00 is duty-free under the SACU Common External Tariff); and mill Certificate of Analysis.

**15. Samples and trial.** Please supply **A4 sheet samples** with the corresponding datasheet, and advise the cost and lead time for a **full trial reel** at our specified trim width for press testing in Gaborone. We will not place a first order without a successful press trial.

**16. Four direct questions, to which we would appreciate written answers.**

1. **Capacity.** Published sources conflict on newsprint capacity at Ngodwana: your own capacity table states **85,000 t/y**, while press reporting in March 2026 cited **110,000 t/y**. Please confirm the correct current figure and your present utilisation.
2. **Continuity.** Please confirm in writing that **newsprint production at Ngodwana is expected to continue for at least the next 12 months**, and state whether the machine is capable of switching to linerboard or other grades. Supply continuity, not price, is our primary risk on this programme.
3. **Channel.** Do you serve Botswana **directly**, or through an appointed merchant or distributor? If through a third party, please provide their details so we approach the correct party.
4. **Contract party.** Please confirm the **registered legal name and CIPC registration number** of the contracting entity, as trade directories show two differing forms.

**17. Long-term supply agreement.** Subject to a satisfactory trial, we wish to conclude a **12-month supply agreement** covering fixed monthly tonnage with call-off flexibility; an agreed price-revision mechanism (we would prefer a quarterly review against a named published index rather than an open right of revision); quality parameters with agreed tolerances and a claims and rejection procedure; and an agreed delivery performance standard. Please indicate your willingness and your standard terms.
