## 2. ВОПРОСЫ НА ВЕРИФИКАЦИЮ (для писем — отсекают посредника-пустышку)
1. Are you the **producer, the title-holder, or a broker** for this parcel? (прямой вопрос о роли)
2. **Current physical location** of the material (warehouse/port) + who holds title now.
3. **Latest independent assay** (lab name, date) — не старше [X] мес; готовы на SGS/Alfred Knight re-assay?
4. **Available tonnage right now** and whether the original parcel is still unsold.
5. **Origin**: mine/plant name, country, export licence / документы происхождения.
6. Price basis (FOB/CIF/DDP), payment terms, готовность к **CIQ/SGS inspection** и escrow/LC.
7. Proof: **stock photos with date**, weighbridge/packing list, warehouse receipt/holding certificate.
