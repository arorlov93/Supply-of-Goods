How we work

Terms a producer can check before replying

  • We take titleWe are the buyer on the contract and the seller on the onward sale. No commission, no introduction fee, no triangular invoicing for the producer to manage.
  • One currency per dealWe buy and sell in the same currency so neither side carries the conversion. Bank charges outside the beneficiary's country are for the payer.
  • Documents through our forwarderWe nominate the forwarder and the bill of lading is issued by them. Health certificates and certificates of origin stay with the producing plant.
  • Trial before programmeEvery new line starts with a trial shipment on full commercial terms. Volume follows performance, not the other way round.
  • Mirrored claim windowsThe inspection and claim period we grant our buyer never exceeds the one we hold with the producer.
  • Plant approval firstWe confirm the establishment number and its approval for the destination country before we place an order, not after.
