# US Customs Import Data — Named Consignees Importing Natural Stone & Ceramic Tile (Turkish origin priority)

**Research date:** 26 September 2026.

**Primary dataset used:** ImportYeti company/supplier reports, built on **U.S. Customs and Border Protection ocean manifest (AMS/BOL) data**, page footer states "Source: U.S. Customs and Border Protection · ImportYeti synced 09/23/2026". Coverage on most company pages runs **01/2015 – 09/2026**; a few pages state a narrower window (noted per company). This is **ocean cargo only** — it excludes air freight and all truck/rail entries from Canada and Mexico.

**Access limitations, stated explicitly (per assignment constraint):**
- `importyeti.com` is behind Cloudflare. Direct `WebFetch` and direct `curl` both returned **HTTP 403 / "Just a moment..." challenge**. All ImportYeti data below was retrieved through a text-extraction proxy (`r.jina.ai`) and cross-read against Google search-index snippets of the same pages.
- The **free tier does not expose port of lading / port of discharge**. That field is **UNKNOWN** for every company below. It also does not expose clean rolling 12-month and 24-month shipment counts — only lifetime totals plus an "Avg. TEU per Month" figure and a monthly bar chart. Where a page gave a narrower date window or a per-month rate, it is reported; otherwise last-12/24-month counts are **NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED in the free view**.
- Four ImportYeti pages returned truncated content on repeated attempts and their metrics could not be read: **Stone Mart Marble & Travertine (Tampa FL)**, **Cosmos Granite East (Raleigh NC)**, **Marble Systems (Fairfax VA)**, **Naturali Stone (Miami FL)**, plus **Stone Line Group**, **Ceramic Matrix**, **Calisco USA**, **Travertine U S Natural Stone** and supplier page **Tureks GM Mermer**. Only the page title/address and, where available, a Google snippet figure could be confirmed for these — flagged individually as UNVERIFIED.
- **Panjiva, ImportGenius, Volza, Datamyne and Descartes** were not usable: all gate manifest-level consignee detail behind paid accounts. No data below comes from them.
- **USITC DataWeb and USA Trade Online** both require login/session handling and returned no queryable HS-level series through this toolchain. Aggregate figures below come from secondary trade press citing Turkstat and from the CTASC/Stone World monthly import series — the underlying Census HS-10 tables were **not** retrieved directly.

**Scope exclusion honoured:** engineered quartz surfaces from Turkey (HTS 6810.99.0020 / 6810.99.0040) are excluded from prospect ranking per the AD/CVD continuation. Where a company's data shows quartz, it is marked.

## Q1. Which US companies appear as consignees on ocean bills of lading for stone and tile FROM TURKEY?

### Takeaway
Manifest data yields a solid, named roster of US consignees receiving Turkish stone and tile at container scale. The heaviest Turkey-dependent buyers are **Stone Hardscapes (Jacksonville FL)**, **Altura Stone and Tile (Norcross GA)**, **Rain Foreign Trade (Miami FL)**, **Emser Tile (West Hollywood CA)**, **Anatolia Tile & Stone (Savannah GA)** and **Monterrey Tile (Chandler AZ)** — several of these are effectively single-origin Turkish operations. Port of discharge is unavailable in every case.

### Inferences
- Turkish stone reaches the US mainly through **three commercial models**: (a) large multi-origin tile houses where Turkey is one of several sources (Emser, Interceramic, Stone Source), (b) **Turkey-only paver/travertine specialists** (Stone Hardscapes, Rain Foreign Trade, Altura, Monterrey Tile), and (c) **captive US arms of Turkish producers** (Turmar Marble Systems ↔ Turmar Ins Mer; Temmer Atlanta ↔ Temmer Mermer). Model (b) is the commercially interesting comparable set; model (c) is closed to third-party suppliers.
- The Turkey-only specialists are concentrated in **outdoor hardscape** (travertine pavers, pool coping, tumbled pavers) rather than interior slabs — visible in Monterrey Tile's HTS 6802.21.x SKU descriptions and Stone Hardscapes' product positioning. That is the product niche Turkey currently owns in the US.
- Several of these buyers are **dangerously single-sourced** (Altura: ~79% of shipments from one shipper, Metamar; Stone Hardscapes: 96% Turkey). That is a supplier-substitution opening, not a greenfield one.

## Q3. Aggregate US import volumes and unit values by HS code, and the trend over 2-3 years

### Takeaway
Direct HS-10 Census/USITC series could not be pulled through this toolchain. The best obtainable substitute is the CTASC/Stone World monthly US countertop-materials import series (June 2026) plus Turkstat-sourced Turkish export data. Both show the same picture: **the US stone import market is growing (+7.4% YoY), marble is the fastest-growing natural category (+12.9%), granite is contracting (-12.2%), and Turkey's share is declining even as Turkey's overall exports to the US rise in value.**

### Cited Findings

- **June 2026, US countertop materials imports: total $367.0M, +7.4% YoY.** Natural stone = **67.7%** of the market; engineered quartz = 32.3% at **$118.4M, +7.8% YoY**. Top five source countries hold **68.2%** combined share: **Brazil $71.0M (-9.4%)**, **Italy $63.4M (+32.2%)**, **Thailand $26.3M (+72.9%)**, India (declining, value not stated), **Turkey (declining, value not stated)**. By stone type: **marble $92.8M (+12.9%)**, **quartzite $60.6M (+9.2%)**, **granite $48.5M (-12.2%)**, dolomite +235% (attributed to Canadian shipment timing). Granite is described as "the only large category still contracting." Data compares June 2026 against June 2025 — [CTASC, citing Stone World article by Jason Kamery, published 2 September 2026](https://ctasc.com/u-s-countertop-materials-imports-june-2026/).
- **HTS-10 detail on the excluded quartz category:** 6810.99.00.20 (engineered quartz jumbo slabs ≥3m × 1.25m) accounted for **$95.4M, i.e. 80.6% of all quartz imports** in June 2026; 6810.99.00.40 covers other engineered-quartz sizes — [CTASC](https://ctasc.com/u-s-countertop-materials-imports-june-2026/).
- **Turkey-side, Jan–Jul 2025:** natural stone exports **3.3 million tons (-2.5% YoY)** worth **US$1,135 million (+5.4% YoY)**, average unit value **US$343.70/ton**. Product split: **marble finished/semi-finished $562.5M (+11.6%)**; **travertine $193.1M (+9.1%)**; **raw materials (blocks) $314.4M (-5.4%)**. Top destinations: **United States $273M (+12.6%)**, China $200M (-8.1%), India $69.9M (-1.5%), Saudi Arabia $59.9M (-10.3%), France $59.2M (+2.3%). Prior-year context: **2024 full-year exports fell 11.7% in volume and 7% in value** — [Marmo Macchine International, citing Turkstat, published 8 February 2026](https://www.marmomacchineinternational.com/en/turkey-natural-stone-exports-up-5-4-in-the-first-7-months-of-2025/).
- **Turkey's total natural stone exports were US$1,784,492,698 in 2023** — [Stone World Türkiye](https://stoneworldtr.com/en/turkeys-natural-stone-exports-reach-1784492698-dollars-in-2023/).
- **HS-code usage in practice:** most processed Turkish tile and slab entering the US is declared under **6802.91 (marble/travertine, polished or honed)** or **6802.93 (granite)**; the US applies a **3.7% duty on HS 6802.91** — [orderfromtr.com buyer's guide](https://orderfromtr.com/blog-importing-stone-turkey). **Low-confidence source (vendor blog, not a tariff authority) — verify against the HTSUS before relying on the duty rate.**
- **CBP has issued binding tariff-classification rulings specifically on natural stone pavers from Turkey**, confirming that this product/origin combination is an established, adjudicated import flow — [CBP ruling N303392](https://rulings.cbp.gov/ruling/N303392).
- **Observed HTSUS 8- and 10-digit codes actually appearing on Turkish-origin BOLs** (from the manifest data in Q1): **6802.21.20 / .30 / .50 / .60 / .70 / .80** (marble, travertine and alabaster — travertine pavers and pool coping, Monterrey Tile); **6802.91.15** (Turmar); **6802.91.90** (marble tiles); **6802.99.90** (pebble tiles); **6907.21.10 / .22 / .26 / .29 / .31 / .32 / .33 / .90 / .93 / .96 / .1020 / .8640 / .9720** (porcelain, water absorption ≤0.5%); **6907.23.32** (water absorption >10%); **6907.30.10** and **6907.40.10** (unglazed / mosaic); **6908.90.90 / .91** (glazed ceramic, legacy code); **6810.99** (Kaleseramik, artificial stone articles) — sources as cited per company in Q1.

### Inferences
- Turkey's exports **to** the US rose 12.6% in value in Jan–Jul 2025 while the CTASC series shows Turkey's share of US **countertop-materials** imports declining in June 2026. These are not necessarily contradictory: the CTASC series covers slabs/countertop materials only, whereas Turkey's US growth is concentrated in **finished marble and travertine tile, pavers and coping** — products largely outside the countertop panel. This is consistent with what the manifest data shows (Turkish flow dominated by 6802.21 pavers, not 6802.93 countertop slabs).
- Turkey's average export unit value of **$343.70/ton** against falling tonnage and rising value indicates a **mix shift from blocks to processed goods** — raw material exports fell 5.4% while finished marble rose 11.6%. That favours US buyers of finished tile/paver rather than block importers.
- Granite's -12.2% contraction alongside marble's +12.9% growth means **granite-focused US importers (Acme Stone, Lackstone, Impex, Atlanta Granite) are in a shrinking category** — which strengthens, not weakens, the case for approaching them with Turkish marble and travertine.

### Gaps
- **USITC DataWeb and USA Trade Online could not be queried.** No direct Census HS-10 series for 2515, 2516, 6802, 6907 or 6810 — neither values, quantities, nor unit values by origin — was retrieved. The numbers above are **secondary trade-press figures**, not the primary Census tables the assignment asked for.
- **No aggregate figures at all were obtained for HS 2515 and 2516 (blocks and rough slabs).** Turkey's "raw materials $314.4M" line is the closest proxy and is not US-specific.
- **No aggregate for 6907 (ceramic/porcelain tile) by origin.** The CTASC series explicitly contains no porcelain-specific data.
- **UN Comtrade and ITC TradeMap were not queried** — both require session/registration handling that this toolchain could not complete.
- **CONFLICTING / REJECTED FIGURE:** a search summary asserted that "Turkey's natural stone exports to the United States increased from USD 490.4 million in June 2025 to USD 693.28 million in June 2026, +41.37%". The underlying Trading Economics pages are for **HS Chapter 71 (pearls, precious and semi-precious stones, metals, coins)** — not natural building stone. **This figure is a misattribution and must not be used.** — [Trading Economics, precious stones](https://tradingeconomics.com/turkey/exports/united-states/precious-semi-precious-stones-not-strung); [Trading Economics, pearls/precious stones/metals](https://tradingeconomics.com/turkey/exports/united-states/pearls-precious-stones-metals-coins).

Afyonkarahisar and Burdur supply the second-tier travertine shippers.
- Because the top Turkish houses already have exclusive-looking relationships with the biggest US buyers, a new Turkish supplier's realistic entry points are the **mid-tier US buyers** (Ceramic Matrix, Ceramic Technics, Meta Marble, Stone Line Group, Natural Stone Deerfield Beach, Allure) and the **no-Turkey prospects** in Q2.

### Gaps
- **Quarry ownership is not identifiable from manifest data.** Whether these exporters own their quarries or trade third-party blocks is **NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED** in this dataset.
- **No production capacity, price or MOQ data** is obtainable from bills of lading.
- Egesan's corporate website could not be confirmed; several smaller shippers (Sorlinen, Stone Terroir, Karden, Aftile, CTM, Ozeller, Fetmer, Sezgin, Dursun, Alfamer, Aymar) were captured only as manifest name strings with no independent second confirmation — **each requires a separate company-register or website check before use.**
- **IMIB's own export-statistics data centre** ([imib.org.tr/en/veri-merkezi/export-statistics/](https://imib.org.tr/en/veri-merkezi/export-statistics/)) was identified as the authoritative Turkish source but its underlying data tables were not retrieved through this toolchain; IMIB does run a **United States Sectoral Trade Delegation** programme ([imib.org.tr](https://imib.org.tr/en/activity/united-states-of-america-sectoral-trade-delegation/)), which is a direct channel worth pursuing manually.
