# Florida Stone and Tile Distributors, Slab Yards, Tile Import Wholesalers and Large Countertop Fabricators

Research date: 26 September 2026. All facts below carry a source URL. Where a fact could not be
confirmed it is written as UNKNOWN / NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED and repeated in the Gaps section.

**Label key used throughout:**
- `CONFIRMED DIRECT IMPORTER` — customs/shipment records or an explicit first-party claim of importing directly from quarries/factories, plus a second independent source.
- `LIKELY BUYER, NO IMPORT EVIDENCE` — scale and product mix fit, but no evidence of direct import found.
- `BUYS DOMESTICALLY ONLY` — evidence the company sources from US distributors.
- `TOO SMALL FOR CONTAINER VOLUME` — single shop / retail showroom, cannot absorb a 40HC per month.
- `BROKER OR FORWARDER, NOT AN END BUYER`
- `NO EVIDENCE`

## Q1. Who are the stone distributors and slab yards in Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, Tampa, Orlando and Jacksonville?

### Takeaway
Florida's slab-yard channel is deep and consolidating. The strongest container-scale prospects for
**Turkish travertine/marble/limestone** are the Tampa cluster (Stone-Mart and Travertine Warehouse,
both with documented Turkish supplier relationships), Corumar in Orlando (a dedicated Turkish-stone
importer), and the multi-branch South Florida slab houses (Opustone, Marble of the World, The Slab
Depot, UMI Stone, Stone Warehouse USA, Armina). Several other big names are US branches of
out-of-state or national importers whose purchasing is centralised outside Florida — they are
volume buyers, but the buying decision is not made in Florida.

### Inferences
- The Tampa 33634 industrial corridor (Anderson Rd / W Hanna Ave / W Knox St) is the densest concentration of container-scale Turkish-stone importers in Florida. Stone-Mart, Travertine Warehouse and Stone Warehouse USA are all within a few miles of each other.
- The Miami slab market skews to Brazilian/Italian/Indian granite and quartzite (Armina, Opustone, Marble of the World), while the Tampa/Central Florida market skews to Turkish travertine and marble for pavers and pool decks. **A Turkish importer should prioritise the West Coast and Central Florida for travertine/limestone and Miami for marble slabs and porcelain.**
- Several of the largest-looking names (MSI, Triton, Cosmos, Marble Systems, Daltile/Keys Granite, The Stone Collection/Marble of the World) are branches of national importers. They are genuine container-scale buyers but the buying decision sits outside Florida — treat them as a separate, corporate-level sales motion.
- Opustone and Armina are confirmed direct importers with published origin lists that **exclude Turkey**. That is the single best qualifying signal in this dataset: they have the infrastructure to import but no incumbent Turkish supplier.

### Gaps
- Warehouse square footage, employee counts and revenue are essentially never published by Florida slab yards. Confirmed square footage was found for only three: Keys Granite (64,777 sq ft), Armina Hialeah (35,000 sq ft, indexed copy only), UMI Manatee (20,000 sq ft).
- No Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) record could be retrieved. Sunbiz's search interface is session/form-based and was not fetchable in this research pass, so **registered legal names, FEI numbers and officer/registered-agent names are UNKNOWN** for every company above except where the trade site itself states an LLC/Corp name (Opustone Stone & Tile Concepts LLC; Stone-Mart Marble & Travertine Group LLC; Travertine Group LLC; Porcemall USA Corp; Gluck GmbH Corp; ITM Tile Importers of Fort Lauderdale Inc; The Slab Depot LLC; Corumar Marble Corporation). **Sunbiz officer lookup is the single highest-value next research step** — it yields legally named owners, which for closely held stone importers are usually also the buyers.

- Membership rosters of the Natural Stone Institute, TCNA and MIA+BSI were not retrievable in this pass; association membership as a scale/credibility filter remains unverified.

Florida has a dense, genuinely container-scale tile import channel concentrated in Doral/Medley
(Miami-Dade) and Deerfield Beach (Broward). Most are already buying full containers from Spain,

## Q3. Who are the large countertop fabricators with multiple shops or their own importing operation?

### Gaps

- Builder-captive fabrication operations (the in-house countertop shops run by large homebuilders and multifamily developers in Florida) were not investigated in this pass and may be the largest hidden container-scale buyers in the state. This is a distinct research thread worth opening.

### Inferences

### Gaps
