# Newsprint Paper Manufacturers in India — Current Producers (verified as of September 2026) and Export Capability to Africa/Botswana

Research date: 25 September 2026. Every claim below carries a source link and, where the source states one, a publication date. Where a fact could not be sourced it is marked **UNKNOWN**, **NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED** or **UNVERIFIED** and moved to Gaps.

Scope note on terminology used throughout: "newsprint" = standard newsprint, typically 42–52 gsm, machine-finished, made wholly or largely from deinked recovered fibre (in India) or mechanical/TMP pulp. Mills producing only writing & printing (maplitho, copier, SS maplitho), coated board, kraft, or bagasse-based printing paper are explicitly excluded from the newsprint list and labelled accordingly.

---

## Q1. Who actually produces newsprint in India today, and what is each mill's current status (ACTIVE / UNCERTAIN / CLOSED / CONVERTED)?

### Takeaway
India has roughly 38 nominally "operational newsprint mills" per the Government of India's DPIIT FY25-26 report (published 18 August 2026), but the segment is structurally shrinking and only a handful of mills are meaningful, verifiable newsprint producers at export scale. The strongest verified current producers are **Emami Paper Mills (Balasore, Odisha)**, **Khanna Paper Mills (Amritsar, Punjab)**, **Nepa Ltd (Nepanagar, MP — restarted PM-1 in April 2026 after a ₹469 cr modernisation)**, **Kerala Paper Products Ltd (Velloor, Kerala — ex-HNL, now diversifying away from newsprint)** and, at small scale, **Malu Paper Mills (Nagpur)**. **Shree Rama Newsprint is effectively dismantling its paper division** and must be treated as NOT A VIABLE SUPPLIER. **TNPL despite its name no longer lists newsprint as a product.**

### Cited Findings — industry-level

- "There are around 38 operational newsprint mills at present in the country." Newsprint represents ~5% of India's paper production by finished-product share — DPIIT (Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Govt of India) report on the paper industry, FY25-26, published 18 August 2026 — [The Pulp and Paper Times summary of DPIIT report](https://thepulpandpapertimes.com/news/dpiit-report-on-the-paper-industry-recent-expansions-upgrades-fastestgrowing-segments-and-total-operational-paper-mills-in-india-4564)
- Newsprint is the **only declining segment** of the Indian paper industry. Per the Indian Newsprint Manufacturers Association (INMA), Indian newspaper demand is ~2.2 million tonnes/year, of which ~68% (1.5 Mt/yr) is met by **imports**, primarily from Canada and Russia — [Parason industry analysis, 2026](https://www.parason.com/blog/future-of-the-paper-industry)
- IPMA member mills reported production of 5,208 thousand tonnes against installed capacity of 5,368 thousand tonnes — 97.01% capacity utilisation — in FY 2025-26. IPMA membership spans "writing, printing, packaging, paperboard, speciality and newsprint" — [IPMA Overview](https://ipmaindia.org/overview-2/); [IPMA About](https://ipmaindia.org/about-ipma/)
- India is a **net importer**, not an exporter, of newsprint: in 2024 India exported only **USD 8.61 million** of newsprint, ranking 24th in the world — [OEC: Newsprint in India Trade](https://oec.world/en/profile/bilateral-product/newsprint/reporter/ind)
- India is the world's second-largest newsprint consumer at ~1.3 million tonnes (2024), with domestic production insufficient to meet demand — [IndexBox India Newsprint Market Report 2026](https://www.indexbox.io/store/india-newprint-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/)

### Inferences
- The commercially realistic shortlist for regular export newsprint from India is **two mills: Emami Paper (Balasore) and Khanna Paper (Amritsar)**, with **Nepa Ltd as a speculative third** once ramp-up is proven and **KPPL as a fourth** if it will entertain export orders alongside its Kerala textbook mandate.
- Because India imports ~68% of its own newsprint demand, Indian mills are structurally **short of newsprint, not long of it**. Export tonnage is therefore opportunistic and price-sensitive rather than a standing programme. An Indian mill is likely to treat a 2–3 container/month Botswana order as marginal business unless domestic demand is soft — which argues for accepting a trading house or agent in the loop, or for benchmarking against South-East Asian / Russian / Canadian origins.
- Every significant Indian newsprint producer identified uses **recycled / deinked recovered fibre, not virgin mechanical pulp** (Nepa 100% recovered paper since 1997; Khanna 390,000 tpa recovered fibre; Emami recycle fibre; KPPL procures ONP). This has direct spec consequences: expect **lower ISO brightness and higher ash than Canadian/Russian virgin newsprint**, and specify brightness/ash/tensile explicitly in any RFQ.
- The 38 "operational newsprint mills" in the DPIIT count is almost certainly counting small mills that make newsprint occasionally as part of a mixed recycled-paper product mix; it should not be read as 38 credible export counterparties.

## Q3. Exports — does the mill export, to where, and is there any Africa / Southern Africa / SADC / Botswana evidence?

### Takeaway
**Khanna Paper Mills is the only Indian newsprint producer with published, company-sourced evidence of exporting to Africa** (Kenya and Egypt named; "SAARC countries, Africa and the Middle East" as stated markets). Emami exports but to **South-East Asia** (board) with OGR speciality outreach to Canada and the Middle East — **no Africa newsprint evidence**. At country level, India's newsprint exports do reach Africa: **Madagascar and Tanzania were the #2 and #4 destinations in 2024.** **No evidence of any Indian newsprint shipment to Botswana, South Africa, Namibia, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Eswatini or Lesotho was found.**

### Cited Findings
- **India's newsprint export destinations, 2024:** Saudi Arabia USD 2.34M, **Madagascar USD 1.71M**, Sri Lanka USD 939k, **Tanzania USD 882k**, UAE USD 440k. Total India newsprint exports USD 8.61M (24th largest exporter globally). Fastest-growing markets 2023→2024: Saudi Arabia (+USD 1.72M), **Madagascar (+USD 918k)**, Sri Lanka (+USD 357k) — [OEC: Newsprint in India Trade](https://oec.world/en/profile/bilateral-product/newsprint/reporter/ind)
- **Volza shipment data (Mar 2023 – Feb 2024):** India exported **157 shipments of newsprint paper** from **41 Indian exporters** to **74 buyers**, a **−41% decline** vs the preceding twelve months — [Volza: Newsprint Paper Exports from India](https://www.volza.com/p/newsprint-paper/export/export-from-india/)
- **KHANNA PAPER — AFRICA EXPORT EVIDENCE (company statement in trade press):** "The company supplies a proportion of their paper volumes to countries like **Sri Lanka, Nepal, Egypt, and Kenya** etc., depending on the business feasibility and the need to balance the existing volumes between domestic and export markets." Distribution reach is "across the globe with a focus on demand markets, **exporting to SAARC countries, Africa and the Middle East**" — [PaperMart interview with Khanna Paper Mills](https://papermart.in/khanna-paper-mills-carves/); see also [PaperMart: "Khanna Paper Mills Limited: Soaring on waste-paper wings"](https://papermart.in/khanna-paper-mills-limited-soaring-on-waste-paper-wings-for-sustainable-paper-business/) and [PaperMart: "Khanna Paper Mills: All for Youthful Synergy"](https://papermart.in/khanna-paper-mills-all-for-youthful-synergy/)

- **TNPL** (printing & writing, not newsprint) exports to "60+ countries" / "100+ global export destinations" — [TNPL](https://tnpl.com/)
- **Kenya as a live African newsprint import market:** a published list of top newsprint importers in Kenya exists via customs data — [ExportGenius: Top 15 Newsprint Paper Importers in Kenya](https://www.exportgenius.in/kenya-importers-of-newsprint-paper)
- **Inbound enquiry evidence:** "Buyers from Canada, **Ethiopia, South Africa**, India, and United Arab Emirates have recently sent inquiries to Indian newsprint manufacturers" — [go4WorldBusiness newsprint suppliers](https://www.go4worldbusiness.com/find?searchText=newsprint&BuyersOrSuppliers=suppliers). **This is a B2B platform activity signal, not proof of completed shipments — treat as weak.**
- **Africa as a growth market for paper generally** — [Africa Pulp & Paper: "Africa: a potential for paper industry"](https://www.africapulpaper.com/en/articles/africa--a-potential-for-paper-industry)
- **Import-side data availability for reverse-checking:** Indian customs-derived import/export shipment records for "Standard Newsprint 45 GSM" are commercially available — [Seair Exim: Standard Newsprint 45 GSM import data](https://www.seair.co.in/standard-newsprint-45-gsm-import-data.aspx)

### Inferences
- The Madagascar and Tanzania figures prove Indian newsprint **can and does move to East/Southern-adjacent Africa by sea in commercial quantities** — the trade lane is real, which de-risks the logistics assumption for Gaborone (transit via Durban, then road to Botswana).
- Khanna Paper's stated Kenya/Egypt business plus its explicit "Africa" market statement makes it the **single best-qualified first approach**. Its 42/45 gsm newsprint grades match the Botswana requirement directly.
- India's newsprint exports **fell 41% year-on-year** and total only ~USD 8.6M. At ~USD 550–650/t that is roughly 13,000–16,000 tonnes/year of Indian newsprint exports **in total, worldwide**. A 2–3 container/month programme (~35–55 t/month, ~420–660 t/yr) would therefore be **3–5% of India's entire newsprint export book** — small enough that any single mill can absorb it, but also small enough that mills may deprioritise it. This also means India is a thin market: a single mill outage removes a large share of supply.
- The absence of any Botswana/South Africa/SADC shipment record is meaningful: the buyer would likely be **opening a new lane**, not joining an existing one. Expect to carry more of the commercial risk (prepayment or LC, no established freight rate, no precedent on claims handling).

### Gaps
- **No evidence found of any Indian newsprint shipment to Botswana, South Africa, Namibia, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Eswatini or Lesotho.** Not found means not found — it does not prove none exist. Paid shipment-database access (Volza / Panjiva / ImportGenius, HS 4801 India→ZA/BW/NA/ZM/ZW/MZ) is required to settle this and was not available in this research.
- 2025 and 2026 India newsprint export figures were not obtainable; the OEC data is 2024 and the Volza window ends February 2024. **Both are OUTDATED relative to September 2026.**
- Whether Emami exports **newsprint** (as opposed to board) at all is **UNVERIFIED**.
