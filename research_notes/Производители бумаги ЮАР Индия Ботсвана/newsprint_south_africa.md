# Newsprint Paper Manufacturers in South Africa — Current Producers (verified as of 25 September 2026)

> Research window: 25 September 2026. All statements carry a source URL and the publication/last-verified date where the source discloses one. Where a fact could not be sourced it is listed under **Gaps** and marked UNKNOWN / NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED.
>
> **Headline conclusion up front:** there is exactly **ONE** verified newsprint-producing mill in South Africa today — **Sappi Ngodwana Mill (Mpumalanga)**. Every other name on the investigation list either never made newsprint, stopped making it years ago, or makes printing/writing, containerboard, kraft or tissue grades only. Mondi Merebank is **no longer a newsprint mill** — its remaining machine makes uncoated woodfree office paper.

---

## Q1. Who are / were the newsprint producers in South Africa?

### Takeaway
As of September 2026 Sappi's Ngodwana Mill is the **only operational newsprint facility in South Africa and in the whole Southern African Customs Union**, a statement made by Sappi itself and reported alongside an ITAC tariff application in March 2026. Mondi's Merebank (Durban) newsprint operation — formerly Mondi Shanduka Newsprint, the historic market leader — has exited newsprint; the site now runs a single uncoated-woodfree machine. No other South African mill (Sappi Tugela, Sappi Stanger, Mpact, Neopak, Corruseal/Enstra, Kimberly-Clark SA, Twinsaver, Nampak, Gayatri, SA Paper Mills) produces newsprint.

### Cited Findings
- "Sappi's Ngodwana mill is **the only operational newsprint facility in South Africa and across the Southern African Customs Union**" — [Business Day, 23 March 2026](https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-03-23-sappi-seeks-5-tariff-on-newsprint-imports-to-protect-local-industry/)
- Sappi has applied to the **International Trade Administration Commission of South Africa (ITAC)** for a **5% import duty on newsprint in rolls or sheets**; ITAC confirmed receipt of the application, which remained under consideration at the time of publication — [Business Day, 23 March 2026](https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-03-23-sappi-seeks-5-tariff-on-newsprint-imports-to-protect-local-industry/)
- Graeme Wild, CEO of Sappi Southern Africa: "the influx of low-priced newsprint imports — often originating from regions with structural overcapacity — has placed significant pressure on local newsprint production" — [Business Day, 23 March 2026](https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-03-23-sappi-seeks-5-tariff-on-newsprint-imports-to-protect-local-industry/)
- Sappi's official Ngodwana mill page states the mill "produces paper pulp for its own consumption, as well as **newsprint**, containerboard and dissolving wood pulp (DWP)" and has been "in continuous operation since 1966" — [Sappi, Ngodwana Mill page, accessed 25 Sep 2026](https://www.sappi.com/en-za/about-us/locations/mills/ngodwana-mill)
- PAMSA (Paper Manufacturers Association of South Africa) member directory: of the seven primary members (Corruseal, Kimberly-Clark SA, Mondi South Africa, Mpact, Neopak, Sappi Southern Africa, Twinsaver), **only Sappi Southern Africa is described as producing newsprint** ("newsprint, graphic and packaging papers"). Mondi South Africa is described as making "pulp, packaging papers, uncoated fine paper, office and professional printing stock, corrugated solutions, and flexible packaging" — **no newsprint** — [PAMSA / The Paper Story member directory, accessed 25 Sep 2026](https://thepaperstory.co.za/about-pamsa/members/)
- Sappi's own newsprint brand page: "Manufactured from **virgin wood fibre**, Sappi Newsprint is a preferred grade amongst local newspaper printers… required strength and runnability for demanding coldset and heatset converting used in the manufacture of newspapers and advertising inserts." The current range shows **1 result: Newsprint Presspride** (with **Superpride** listed as a related product) — [Sappi Newsprint product page, accessed 25 Sep 2026](https://www.sappi.com/en-za/products-services/products/newsprint)

### Inferences
- Because Sappi is publicly asking government for protection against newsprint imports and is the only SACU producer, a Botswana buyer has exactly one domestic-to-SACU mill option; everything else marketed as "South African newsprint" will be either imported paper resold by a trader, or a different grade mislabelled as newsprint.
- Botswana is inside SACU, so a 5% ITAC newsprint duty (if granted) would raise the landed cost of *third-country* newsprint into Gaborone too, improving Sappi's relative position on this lane. This is an inference from SACU's common external tariff, not a statement in the source.

### Gaps
- The ITAC application reference/case number, the exact tariff heading applied for (presumably HS 4801), the comment deadline and current status after March 2026 were not retrievable — no ITAC notice or Government Gazette text was found in search. **UNVERIFIED after 23 March 2026.**

### Inferences
- Merebank's trajectory is: 3 newsprint machines → 1 closed → 1 closed (announced in the Mondi cost-cutting round, recorded in the 2013 accounts) → newsprint exited; then a 70,000 t UCWF machine stopped in H2 2019; the site today runs **one** machine at 270,000 t of uncoated woodfree. The mill was **not sold and not closed** — it is still owned and operated by Mondi. So the correct label for Merebank newsprint is **CLOSED (machines shut/decommissioned), site repurposed to office paper** — not "converted to containerboard". I found **no evidence at all** of a Merebank newsprint-to-containerboard conversion; the Mondi containerboard conversion widely reported in 2025 is at **Duino, Italy** (LWC → 420,000 t recycled containerboard), not Merebank — [Mondi, Duino start-up, 2025](https://www.mondigroup.com/news-and-insight/2025/mondi-duino-marks-start-up-of-recycled-containerboard-mill-with-international-customers-in-italy/); [Voith press release, 17 Dec 2025](https://www.voith.com/corp-en/news-room/press-releases/2025-12-17_vp-mondi-brings-recycled-containerboard-machine-online.html). Any claim that Merebank newsprint became containerboard is most likely a conflation with Duino.
