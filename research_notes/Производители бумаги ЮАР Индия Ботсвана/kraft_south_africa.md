**Research date: 25 September 2026.** All statements carry a source and, where the source is dated, that date. Where a fact could not be sourced it is written as UNKNOWN / NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED and repeated in the Gaps section. Nothing in this file is estimated or inferred unless it appears under "Inferences".

**Method caveat up front:** several key manufacturer sites (Sappi product/mill pages at `sappi.com/en-za/...` and `sappi.com/sappi-paper-and-paper-packaging`, `enstrapaper.co.za`) returned HTTP 403/503 to automated fetching during this session. Findings for those companies therefore rest on (a) Sappi pages that did resolve, (b) equipment-supplier press releases (Bellmer, Voith, ANDRITZ, Valmet), and (c) the industry directory paper-world.com. **No published technical datasheet (burst / ring crush / Cobb / moisture), reel width, reel diameter, core diameter, reel weight, or MOQ was obtained from any South African mill in this session.** That is the single biggest gap in this dossier and is flagged throughout.

## Q1. Who actually produces kraft paper in South Africa? (Classification of every company investigated)

### Takeaway
South Africa has a small, highly concentrated kraft/containerboard manufacturing base. Only **two mills are confirmed virgin (fresh-fibre) kraft producers — Sappi Ngodwana (brown kraftliner, 140–400 gsm) and Mondi Richards Bay (Baywhite white-top kraftliner, 115–250 gsm)**. Everything else is **recycled-fibre** testliner / fluting / bag grades / coreboard (Mpact Felixton and Mkhondo, Corruseal Enstra, Neopak Rosslyn, Golden Era/Gayatri, Continental Paper & Packaging, South African Paper Mills). Several names in the original brief are wrong or out of date: **"Mondi Felixton" and "Mondi Springs" no longer exist as Mondi assets — they are Mpact**; **Nampak is no longer a paper-mill operator**; **Mpact Springs is cartonboard, not kraft, and is being shut down in 2026**.

## Q3. The product-definition question: do NOT conflate these kraft products

### Takeaway
"Kraft paper" in a South African RFQ can mean six materially different products. Confusing them is the most likely cause of a failed order. The dividing lines that matter commercially are **fibre basis (virgin vs recycled)**, **function (liner vs medium vs wrapping/bag)**, and **top-ply (brown vs white-top)**.

### Cited Findings and definitions
- **Kraft paper (generic)** — the parent category, paper from the kraft (sulphate) chemical pulping process — [Wikipedia, Kraft paper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraft_paper)
- **Virgin / fresh-fibre kraft** — made from fresh chemical wood pulp. Highest strength per gram, cleanest, best for food contact and for demanding bags. In South Africa: **Sappi Ngodwana** (unbleached softwood base, integrated kraft pulp — [Bellmer](https://www.paper-world.com/en/newsdetail/bellmer-upgrade-of-sappi-ngodwana-mill)) and **Mondi Richards Bay ProVantage Baywhite, "100% fresh fibre"** — [Mondi](https://www.mondigroup.com/en/products-and-solutions/containerboard/containerboard-products/provantage-baywhite/).
- **Recycled kraft / testliner** — made from recovered paper. Lower strength at equal grammage, cheaper, adequate for most dry secondary packaging. In South Africa: **Mpact Felixton "exclusively recycled fibre", FSC Recycled 100%** ([Mpact](https://www.mpact.co.za/our-products/paper-business/paper-manufacturing)); **Neopak "100% waste-based fibres"** ([Neopak](https://www.neopak.co.za/)); **Enstra "100% recycled kraft"**; **Gayatri "kraft paper from recycled kraft paper"** ([Golden Era](https://www.golden-era.co.za/new-era-plastics/)).
- **Semi-virgin** — a documented middle category in South Africa: **Mpact Mkhondo is FSC Mix 70%** and makes **semi-chemical fluting** ([Mpact](https://www.mpact.co.za/our-products/paper-business/paper-manufacturing)); **Sappi Tugela uses "both recycled and virgin fibres"** and internally produces 170,000 t/y NSSC semi-chemical pulp ([Sappi Tugela](https://www.sappi.com/tugela-mill)). Semi-chemical fluting is specifically valued for wet/cold-storage stiffness.
- **Kraftliner vs Testliner** — both are the flat outer/inner facings of corrugated board; **kraftliner = predominantly virgin kraft pulp; testliner = predominantly recovered fibre.** In SA: kraftliner = Sappi Ngodwana KLB and Mondi Baywhite; testliner = Mpact Felixton (explicitly "fluting and testliner 90–170 g/m²" — [Bellmer, Jun 2024](https://www.bellmer.com/2024/06/18/dryness-and-quality-on-point/)).
- **Fluting / corrugating medium** — the wavy middle ply, not a facing. Buying fluting when you need liner (or vice versa) gives the wrong stiffness/flat-crush profile. SA fluting producers: Sappi Tugela (Ultraflute Plus, Ultraflex), Mpact Felixton, Mpact Mkhondo (semi-chemical), Neopak (Recycled Fluting, Powerflute).
- **White-top kraftliner vs brown kraftliner** — white-top has a bleached top ply for print quality and shelf appeal. **Mondi Baywhite: "top sheet of bleached eucalyptus kraft pulp, base sheet containing unbleached softwood fibre"**, sold on "high-quality printing and shelf appeal" for FFV, dairy, beverages, eCommerce — [Mondi ProVantage Baywhite](https://www.mondigroup.com/en/products-and-solutions/containerboard/containerboard-products/provantage-baywhite/). Brown kraftliner (Sappi Ngodwana KLB, Sappi KraftPride "3 ply virgin linerboard… for agricultural and industrial environments" — [Sappi](https://www.sappi.com/en-us/products-services/products/kraftpride)) is cheaper and suits industrial/unprinted use.
- **Sack kraft vs packaging kraft** — sack kraft is a distinct extensible, high-porosity, high-TEA grade for valve/multiwall bags (cement, feed, flour). Industry-typical grammage for sack kraft is **60–100 gsm** — [Market Growth Reports, Sack Kraft Paper Market](https://www.marketgrowthreports.com/market-reports/sack-kraft-paper-market-114469). **No South African mill was found publishing sack kraft production.** Closest domestic offers are **Enstra "Paper Bag Materials — lightweight papers for fast food, shopping bags, and stationery"** ([Corruseal](https://corruseal.co.za/enstra-paper/)) and **Neopak bag grades** — these are recycled bag kraft, not extensible sack kraft.
- **Specialty kraft** — Neopak "Speciality Papers" and **NeoCore** for spiral-wound cores/tubes, "double-sided sized quality" (tissue, textile, plastic film, stationery); Enstra security papers (passports, election documents, cheques) — [Neopak](https://www.neopak.co.za/); [Corruseal Enstra](https://corruseal.co.za/enstra-paper/).

### Inferences (practical guidance for the buyer)
- If the Botswana application is **corrugated box making**, the buyer needs to specify liner **or** medium, and will find Mpact Felixton (90–170 gsm recycled testliner/fluting) and Sappi Ngodwana (140–400 gsm virgin kraftliner) the two natural fits.
- If the application is **bags / wrapping (Segments A–B)**, the only domestic options are **Corruseal Enstra bag grades** and possibly **Neopak** and **SA Paper Mills wrapping papers** — all recycled, none with published specs. Genuine extensible sack kraft will probably have to be imported.
- If the application is **tubes and cores (Segment E)**, **Neopak NeoCore** is the named commercial product, with Sappi Ngodwana KLB up to 400 gsm as a heavy-liner alternative.
- If **print quality matters**, only **Mondi Baywhite** (white-top, 115–250 gsm) is a domestic white-top kraftliner.

### Gaps
- No published technical datasheet values (burst index, ring crush / SCT, Cobb60, moisture %, CMT for fluting) were obtained for **any** South African kraft grade this session. Mondi's own ProVantage Baywhite page states specifications are available only by contacting Mondi. This must be requested directly from every shortlisted mill.

## Q9. Logistics to Gaborone

### Takeaway
**Direct road delivery from a South African mill to Gaborone is not only realistic, it is the standard method** — Botswana is landlocked, shares a border with South Africa a short drive from Gaborone, and is in SACU with South Africa. **No seaport is needed for a South African origin.** The Gauteng mills (Corruseal Enstra/Springs, Neopak/Rosslyn, Continental/Wadeville) and Gayatri/Mogwase are the shortest hauls.

### Cited Findings
- **Gaborone "is the country's capital, located near the southern border with South Africa"**; the **Gaborone Container Terminal supports warehousing and storage of containers** — [Transport in Botswana, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_in_Botswana); [DSV Botswana](https://www.dsv.com/en/countries/africa/botswana)
- **Standard practice for overseas cargo: "landed in South Africa and forwarded under RIB (Removal in Bond) by road to Gaborone warehouse for further clearing and final delivery."** Daily transport services operate South Africa → Botswana and to outlying areas of Botswana — [DSV Botswana](https://www.dsv.com/en/countries/africa/botswana)
- **Road is the cheapest mode Durban → Gaborone, with total transit ~4 days including loading and unloading** — [CargoRouter, Durban→Gaborone](https://www.cargorouter.com/freight-shipping/South-Africa/Durban/Botswana/Gaborone/). This is the worst-case route (KZN mills: Sappi Tugela, Mondi Richards Bay, Mpact Felixton, SA Paper Mills/Durban).
- **In-country capability:** DSV Botswana operates a **400 m² free and 100 m² bonded warehouse in Commerce Industrial Park, Gaborone** — [DSV Botswana](https://www.dsv.com/en/countries/africa/botswana)
- Botswana freight-forwarder options are numerous — [Freight Forwarder Services, Botswana](https://freightforwarderservices.com/botswana/); [Freightnet Botswana](https://www.freightnet.com/directory/p1/cBW/s99.htm)

### Inferences (clearly labelled — geography and SACU status were not sourced from a primary document this session)
- **Gauteng is the right origin.** Springs, Rosslyn (Pretoria) and Wadeville are all roughly 350–400 km from Gaborone by road via the Tlokweng/Kopfontein or Pioneer Gate border posts — a **same-day or next-day single-truck delivery**, versus ~4 days from Durban. **Corruseal Enstra (Springs), Neopak (Rosslyn) and Continental Paper & Packaging (Wadeville) are therefore the logistically optimal kraft mills for a Gaborone buyer.**
- **Gayatri Paper Mills at Mogwase, North West** is the closest kraft mill of all to the Botswana border (Mogwase is north-west of Rustenburg) — worth a call despite its thin public documentation.
- **Botswana and South Africa are both members of the Southern African Customs Union (SACU)**, which means intra-SACU movement of South African-origin goods is duty-free and needs no customs duty payment, only VAT/administration. This materially favours a South African origin over an Indian or European one for a Botswana buyer. **This is well-established public fact but I did not fetch a primary SACU/SARS source in this session — verify before relying on it in a commercial document.**
- **Rail** exists (Botswana Railways connects to Transnet), but for one 20–25 t container a month, **road is the only sensible mode** — consolidation, frequency and door delivery all favour it, and the sources above show daily road services already running.
- **Because the origin is inside SACU and the destination is inland, "container" should be read as a road truck/trailer load, not a maritime container.** A single truck can carry ~28–34 t of paper reels in South Africa, so "1 container/month" is likely one truckload — worth clarifying with the buyer, because it changes whether a mill sees the order as 20 t or 34 t.

### Gaps
- Actual road distances and transit times mill-by-mill were not sourced; only the Durban→Gaborone benchmark (4 days, road cheapest) is cited.
- No SACU/SARS primary document fetched; duty-free status asserted as general knowledge and flagged.
- Border-post throughput, permit requirements for cross-border road haulage (SADC cross-border permits), and whether any of these mills delivers DDP Gaborone vs ex-works only: **all UNKNOWN**.
