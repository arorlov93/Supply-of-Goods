## SCOPE OF WORK
ISP Group will provide post-construction cleaning to deliver the space **broom-clean through
final/detail clean, ready for owner turnover**:

**Rough clean (during/after trades):**
- Remove construction debris, trash, and materials from all areas
- Sweep/vacuum floors; remove stickers, labels, tape, and adhesive residue
- Wipe down horizontal surfaces; remove gross dust

**Final / detail clean (before CO):**
- Detail-clean all fixtures, casework, cabinetry, doors, frames, hardware, switch plates
- Clean interior glass, windows, mirrors, and partitions (both sides, low-rise)
- Clean and sanitize restrooms and kitchens/break rooms (fixtures, counters, floors)
- Damp-mop / machine-scrub hard floors; vacuum carpet; polish as specified
- Dust vents, light fixtures, ledges, and exposed surfaces
- Final touch-up walk with your superintendent
