### Technical Approach Narrative (полный — требование оценки)

**1. Understanding of scope.** Exterior surface maintenance and painting of the ERRC Vehicle Service Building: CMU structure approx. 40′×55′×14′ (~2,600 SF of exterior wall surface), including repair of approx. 45 LF of small cracks, surface preparation, priming, and application of a complete exterior coating system per manufacturer requirements and industry standards (SSPC/PDCA guidelines).

**2. Sequence of work.**
- Mobilization; site protection (drop cloths, masking of doors/windows/fixtures, protection of adjacent surfaces and vehicles)
- Surface preparation: pressure washing to remove dirt/chalking/loose coatings; scraping/wire-brushing of failing paint; cleaning per SSPC-SP1
- Crack repair: rout/clean ~45 LF of cracks; fill with elastomeric masonry sealant/patching compound; tool flush
- Spot-priming of repairs and bare CMU; full prime coat (masonry primer/block filler as required by substrate condition)
- Finish: two coats premium exterior 100% acrylic masonry coating, spray-and-back-roll application; manufacturer-specified DFT and recoat windows
- Daily cleanup; final inspection with COR; punch list; demobilization
- Weather limits per manufacturer (temp/humidity/rain windows); work coordinated with facility operations to avoid disruption

**3. Personnel & supervision.** Working supervisor on site at all times; 2–3 person crew; all workers paid per Davis-Bacon WD PA20260099; certified payrolls submitted weekly.

**4. Quality control.** Surface-prep verification before coating; wet-film thickness checks; manufacturer data sheets and color submittals for Government approval before application.

**5. Safety.** Site-specific safety plan (ladders/staging up to 14 ft, OSHA 1926 Subpart X; PPE; SDS on site). No hazardous abatement anticipated; any suspect coatings reported to CO before disturbance.

**6. Schedule.** Estimated 5–7 working days on site; start within 10 days of notice to proceed, weather permitting.
